Steam Transformer Design Software

Clean steam from contaminated steam

A steam transformer system is the method power plants use to transform high energy contaminated steam into clean steam, which can then be used for other purposes in the plant. The program performs the thermal and mechanical design and sizing of the whole system, and the results give the thermal and dimensional data of each unit.

The system combines several pieces of equipment:

  • Desuperheater – reduces the temperature of the steam before it goes to the steam transformer.
  • Desuperheater tank – at the desuperheater outlet, where the steam is prepared for use.
  • Steam transformer – where the clean steam is produced.
  • Drain cooler – cools the condensate of the heating steam, transferring its heat to the water that will be vaporized.
  • Deaerator tank – deaerates the water, heating it with a fraction of the steam produced in the steam transformer.

If the steam is not superheated, the system has only three units and the desuperheater and its tank are not needed.

Data entry

The thermal window collects the generator steam conditions (flow, pressure, temperature, pressure drops), the produced steam conditions, the water conditions, tube diameters and thicknesses, tube materials and allowable stresses. A second window collects the mechanical design data of each unit.

Results for each unit

The results give, for the desuperheater, its tank, the deaerator, the steam transformer and the drain cooler: flows, temperatures and pressures on each side, exchanged load, LMTD, exchange coefficient and surface, number and material of tubes, shell and channel diameters and thicknesses, nozzle diameters, number of support plates and length of equipment.
