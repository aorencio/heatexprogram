Heaters for district heating systems

This program performs the thermal and mechanical design of heaters for a district heating system, based on HEI standards and heat transfer coefficients from classic correlations. Horizontal units are accommodated, and especially vertical units. The program also generates a heater drawing with the principal dimensions of the unit and of the tube support system, the tube layout, and the results sheet for thickness and weights.

Data entry

The input window collects the steam flow, temperature, pressure and enthalpy, desuperheater data, drains, drain outlet temperature, heated water flow, pressure and inlet and outlet temperatures, tube side design pressure, tube diameter, thickness and pitch, water velocity, tube material, type of installation (horizontal or vertical channel down), correction coefficients, number of tube passes and type of water box.

Thermal results

The results sheet gives the duty, LMTD, heat transfer coefficient and effective surface of each zone, number of U tubes, tube side pressure drop, terminal temperature difference, drain cooler approach, shell and channel diameters, heater length, nozzle diameters and the number of support plates and pressure drop of each zone.

Drawings

Mechanical calculation and weights

The mechanical module calculates the thickness of the shell, transition piece, tube sheet, water box and cover, support plates, expansion joint and pipes, and the weights of the main parts, up to the heater weight empty, in operation and full of water.
