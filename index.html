Steam Surface Condenser

For thermal and mechanical design and sizing of steam surface condensers based on heat transfer coefficients in accordance with HEI standards. This program is valid for small cylindrical units or larger condensers used in thermal and nuclear power plants. Other separate and complementary programs exist for the specific internal systems. See full details and screenshots.

Steam Surface Condenser Performance

Based on data taken during the condenser performance test, this program will calculate the expected steam test pressure and compare this value with the actual measured value to indicate the tube fouling coefficient and the ineffective surface as a percentage of the total. A complementary program for performance curves also exists. See full details and screenshots.

Dual Pressure Condenser

Similar to the Steam Surface Condenser program, but applicable for different turbine pressure in each shell. Details of the connection between hotwells and turbine necks are given. See full details and screenshots.

Condenser Revamping

Specifically adapted for the modular renovation of steam surface condensers through complete replacement of the tube bundles, this program calculates the performance of the new unit and corresponding production gains for a one year period from turbine and cooling water pump characteristics. See full details and screenshots.

Feedwater Heater

Thermal and mechanical design of feedwater heaters based on HEI standards and heat transfer coefficients for each zone based on classic correlations (Nusselt, McAdams,…) or proprietary methods. Full or split flow heaters are accommodated, as are horizontal and vertical units with up and down channel. In addition to the performance results required to complete heater specification datasheet, the program also generates a heater drawing showing the principal dimensions of the unit and of the tube support system, tube layout and results sheet for thickness and weights. See full details and screenshots.

Feedwater Heater Performance

Performance analysis of an existing feedwater heater based on measured performance test data. The program evaluates the performance of each zone of the heater and indicates the percentage of inactive surface in each zone, suggesting the possible cause of poor or inadequate performance where predicted. See full details and screenshots.

Desuperheater

Conceived specifically for desuperheating units, it is a special heater with only a desuperheating surface. The thermal and mechanical design is similar to the previous program. See full details and screenshots.

District Heating

Thermal and mechanical design of heaters for a District Heating System based on HEI standards and heat transfer coefficients based on classic correlations. Heaters are accommodated as horizontal and specially for vertical units. In addition the program also generates a heater drawing showing the principal dimensions of the unit and of the tube support system, tube layout and results sheet for thickness and weights. See full details and screenshots.

Natural Gas Heater

Thermal and mechanical design of shell and tube heat exchanger used to heat Natural Gas before using it as fuel for gas turbines in combined-cycle power stations. These units heat the gas to a high temperature using medium-pressure superheated steam with three surfaces (desuperheating, condensation, and drain cooler in a same shell, similar to traditional feedwater heaters at power plants). See full details and screenshots.

Steam Transformer

Thermal and mechanical design and sizing of steam/steam transformation systems, in which steam at a specific conditions is obtained from steam at other different conditions. The corresponding results give the thermal and dimensional data of each unit in the system. See full details and screenshots.

Water Heat Exchanger

Thermal design and mechanical calculation of water/water heat exchanger for applications in power stations. See full details and screenshots.

Deaerator

For thermal and mechanical design and sizing of deaerators. This program is valid for small industrial units, medium vertical units or larger horizontal used in industries and thermal or nuclear power plants. See full details and drawings.

In-line Feedwater Heaters Performance

Performance calculation of a complete battery of in-line feedwater heaters for a maximum of six heaters (LP & HP) in-line with cascade drain type, reverted by booster pump or lost to the outside. See full details and screenshots.
