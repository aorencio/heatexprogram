Natural Gas Heater Design Software

Thermal and mechanical design of steam heated natural gas heaters for gas turbines in combined-cycle power stations.

Home › Natural Gas Heater

Gas heaters for combined-cycle power stations

This program performs the thermal and mechanical design of shell and tube heat exchangers used to heat natural gas before it is used as fuel in the gas turbines of combined-cycle power stations. These units heat the gas to a high temperature with medium-pressure superheated steam, using three surfaces in the same shell (desuperheating, condensing and drain cooling), similar to the traditional feedwater heaters of power plants.

Gas properties

First, the user enters the gas density, dynamic viscosity, heat capacity and thermal conductivity at six temperatures at the nominal gas pressure. The program fits a polynomial to each property, so the calculation uses the right value at every temperature.

Data entry

The main window collects the steam flow, temperature, pressure and enthalpy, pressure drop at the desuperheater, drain outlet temperature, gas flow, inlet and outlet temperature and pressure, tube diameter, thickness and pitch, gas velocity, tube material, fouling resistances, type of installation, water box type and number of tube passes.

Results

The results sheet gives the duty, LMTD, heat transfer coefficient and effective surface of each zone, number of U tubes, tube side pressure drop, gas temperatures in each zone, terminal temperature difference, drain cooler approach, shell and channel dimensions, nozzle diameters, support plates and dew point verification.

Mechanical calculation and performance

The mechanical module gives the thickness and weights of the main parts. A performance module calculates the expected results of the designed heater at other steam and gas conditions.
